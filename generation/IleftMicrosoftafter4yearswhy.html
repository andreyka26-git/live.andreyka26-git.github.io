Reasons to leave

Many of you might ask - why then?

Money

What a surprise, right? As a person who got to Microsoft from Ukrainian outsource companies I was very surprised by how FAANGs are paying you.

Disclaimer: I DO NOT talk about Microsoft, but rather about general approach in all  tier1/ tier2 companies.

Total compensation = base salary + on-hire stocks + yearly stock bonus + yearly cache bonus (optional)

Base salary

Pretty simple you are getting some amount of money and paying local country taxes for it. Mostly Microsoft is working with you as an employee (not self employed) so taxes are pretty high.

On-hire stock

This is the most tasty piece of cake. How it works:

  • company is “buying” you stock for some static amount of money, let’s say they bought you 100 shares and paid 2k EUR according to current stock price. You don’t own stocks yet.
  • The company starts transferring you these 100 shares part by part over a 4 years period, usually. This process is called vesting. Typically they divide 100 by quarters and pay you some part each quarter. Some companies might delay first vesting by one year or so - to keep incentive for you to stay.

The main profit here is that stock might grow while it is not yours still. Let’s say the original 100 shares increased in price 3 times. By the end of 4 years you will have 6k EUR instead of initial 2k.

Yearly bonus

Sounds like most of the people would just go, work for 4 years and leave, no?

Actually, no. There is another incentive for company to keep you

Typically FAANGs have yearly bonuses based on your performance. It works exactly like an on-hire stock bonus. Some people call it “stock refresher”. The company assigns another portion of stock for you for the next 4 years. The only difference is that this bonus depends on your performance and it is bounded by the range that is defined by your level.


Let’s say, juniors have range 0-500 eur stock award, mids 0-1000, seniors 0-2000. The higher level you have - the better stock you get.

During your 4 years of on-hire stocks - you accumulate these yearly stock in the way that by the end of on-hire stocks - you kind of stay with the same amount of money.

Note that the most “profitable” is actually your last on-hire stocks bonus. After that you lower stock compensation by two.

Why do people in general stay longer? Because of promotion. In 4 years you will get much better stocks if you go up 2-3 levels, so the result won’t be 1k as in the picture.

For me jun vs mid stocks were not that big of a difference.

FAANGs reinforce leaving over giving promotion

I still don’t understand why. The general rule in FAANGs is that if you are growing within the company you are getting paid less compared to people on your level that just joined.

Consider an example:

  • junior that over 4 years grew up to senior
  • lately joined senior

Typically it works in the way that a new hire senior will make much more than you. He would have a higher base most probably, and higher stocks.

From a company perspective, a junior that grows to senior has MUCH MORE value than just joining one, as he knows how everything works, familiar with the project, team, processes.

But still FAANG will pay the new hire more. This works like that as the more you are promoting - the closer you to median on your level.

That’s why it is VERY bad to join as a bit overqualified junior compared to underqualified mid.
